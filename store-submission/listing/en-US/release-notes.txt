The first release on Google Play, for Googlebooks: more than 100 PDF tools in a sidebar, with search (Ctrl+K) and your recent tools. Organize pages in the PDF Multi Tool, edit text, sign, fill in forms, convert office documents to PDF and PDFs to documents and spreadsheets, make scans searchable, compress and encrypt, all on your device without an internet connection. Open PDFs from the Files app or share files to PDF Toolbox; results go to your Download folder.
