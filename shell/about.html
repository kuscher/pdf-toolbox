BentoPDF {{bentopdf_version}}, the private PDF toolkit, as an offline app for Googlebooks.

A passion project

PDF Toolbox is a personal passion project by Fika Labs. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any employer, and nothing here speaks for one. It isn't made or endorsed by the BentoPDF authors or by Google either.

It is developed on a Googlebook, in the Googlebook's built-in Linux Terminal: the Terminal's Debian builds the app, and the Googlebook tests it on itself.

With a little help from Claude.

Built on BentoPDF

BentoPDF is copyright © the BentoPDF authors and licensed under the GNU AGPL v3. PDF Toolbox contains a modified version of BentoPDF {{bentopdf_version}} (commit {{bentopdf_commit_short}}): built in Simple Mode with every engine served from the app instead of the internet, with PDF Toolbox's name and logo in its header, and with PDF Toolbox's page script, which adds opening and saving files, printing and a compact tool layout. PDF Toolbox's sidebar shows its pages in place of BentoPDF's top bar. The PDFs you make keep BentoPDF's producer line.

Privacy

PDF Toolbox has no internet permission. Your files are processed on this device and never leave it. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or crash reports. Links to websites open in your browser.
